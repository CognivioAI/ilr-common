# Playbook: Employment

For questions touching hiring, discipline, dismissal, or restrictive covenants involving
the platform's own team. This is a narrow, occasional-use playbook — Cognivio AI is a small
team and most matters here should go to counsel quickly rather than being analyzed deeply
in-house.

## Missing-facts checklist (ask before analyzing — see `reasoning/issue-spotting.md`)

- Length of continuous employment (unfair-dismissal qualifying period, currently 2 years
  for standard unfair dismissal claims — verify current threshold before relying on it,
  `research/legal-research-protocol.md`)
- Jurisdiction (E&W / Scotland / NI — employment law differs)
- Reason for the proposed action
- Any protected characteristic potentially in play (Equality Act 2010 discrimination risk)
- Disciplinary/performance process followed so far, and whether it was consistent
- Collective consultation obligations (redundancy scenarios, headcount thresholds)
- Relevant contract terms (notice, PILON, restrictive covenants)
- Union or other representation status

## Issue checklist

- Unfair dismissal (qualifying period, fairness of process and reason)
- Discrimination (protected characteristics, direct/indirect, victimisation)
- Redundancy (genuine redundancy situation, selection criteria, consultation)
- Restrictive covenant enforceability (reasonableness of scope, duration, geography)
- Whistleblowing (Public Interest Disclosure Act protection, if a disclosure is in the
  history)
- Notice and pay in lieu

## Escalation trigger

Any actual or proposed dismissal, disciplinary action, or redundancy — route to named
employment counsel before any action is taken, not after. This playbook exists to prepare
a tight brief for that counsel (`counsel-handoff.md`), not to reach a conclusion in-house.

## Output

A missing-facts list plus an issue-spotted summary, packaged as a counsel brief. Never a
"you can/can't terminate this person" conclusion.
